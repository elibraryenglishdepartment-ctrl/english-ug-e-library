Academic Writing Quick Guide

Paragraph

Begin with a clear topic sentence. Develop one main idea with explanation and textual evidence. End by linking the paragraph back to the argument.

Thesis

State a specific, arguable interpretation. Each body paragraph should contribute directly to the thesis.

Evidence

Introduce quotations, explain their wording and connect them to your claim. Avoid dropping quotations into a paragraph without analysis.

Literary Terms Starter Glossary

Metaphor

A comparison in which one thing is presented as another to create meaning or emphasis.

Imagery

Language that creates sensory or mental impressions for the reader.

Irony

A contrast between expectation and reality, or between literal and intended meaning.

Symbolism

An object, action or image suggesting meanings beyond its literal function.

Theme

A significant idea or concern explored by a literary work.

Point of View

The narrative position from which a story is presented.

Close-Reading Checklist

Ask: What is happening? How is it written? Which words, images or structures matter? What changes? What does the passage suggest about character, theme or context? Which short quotation best proves the interpretation?
